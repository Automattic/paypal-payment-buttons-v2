## Recording Notes

- Use a clean WordPress install with Twenty Twenty-Five theme
- Site name: "My Shop" or similar
- Hide the admin bar on frontend shots
- Use sandbox credentials for the recording (real connection, fake money)
- Capture at 1080p, 30fps
- Mouse movements should be deliberate and slightly slow (viewers need to follow)
- Pause 0.5s after each click before moving to the next action
- Add subtle click sounds for each button press (optional)

## Captions

- White text on semi-transparent dark background
- Bottom center of screen
- Font: system sans-serif, 18px
- Auto-hide after 3 seconds

## Music

- Soft, upbeat background track (royalty-free)
- Low volume — captions do the explaining
- Fade out on closing card
